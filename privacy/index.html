1. Who we are

Lanterno Studio (“Lanterno”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a web design business based in Australia, owned and operated by Bilal Shaikh. We design and build websites for businesses around the world.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and protect personal information when you visit lanternostudio.com (the “Site”), contact us, receive a homepage preview or other business communication from us, or engage us to build a website. It also explains the rights you have and how to use them.

For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (together, the “GDPR”) and similar laws, Lanterno is the controller of the personal information described in this policy, except where section 12 says we act on a client’s behalf.

Although some small businesses are exempt from the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), we choose to handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles. Depending on where you live, other privacy laws may also apply, including the GDPR and the privacy laws of individual US states. Where a law gives you more protection than this policy describes, that law applies.

You can contact us about privacy at any time at hello@lanternostudio.com.

2. The short version

  • We collect only what we need to reply to you, prepare and send relevant business proposals, and deliver the work you engage us for.
  • The Site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or third-party trackers.
  • When you send our contact form, we also record the country your connection comes from, as a two-letter code. We do not record your precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or stop hearing from us, at any time.

The rest of this policy gives the detail.

3. What we collect

3.1 Information you give us through the contact form

When you send the contact form on the Site, we collect:

  • your first name, last name and email address;
  • your current website address, if you choose to give it; and
  • anything you write in the free-text field about what you would like your website to do.

At the same time, we automatically record the date and time of your submission, the address of the page you sent it from, and the country associated with your internet connection (see section 3.4).

3.2 Information you give us in other ways

If you email us or otherwise communicate with us, we collect the information you choose to include, such as your name, contact details, business details and the content of your message.

3.3 Information about clients

If you engage us, we also collect information needed to deliver the work and run our business, such as your name, business name, billing and contact details, records of payments and correspondence, project content (for example text, images and branding you provide), and the access details you give us for your domain, hosting or other accounts. Payments are processed by our payment and banking providers. We do not store full payment card numbers.

3.4 Information collected automatically

Country of your connection. When you open the contact form, your browser makes a request to the domain that receives our form submissions. That domain is served through Cloudflare, which returns the country associated with your IP address as a two-letter code (for example “AU”). We include that code with your submission so we know which market you are in. Your IP address is processed by Cloudflare to do this, but we do not store your IP address with your submission, and we do not determine your precise location.

Server logs. Like most websites, the services that host the Site and receive form submissions automatically log technical information when you visit or submit a form, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the date and time. These logs are used to deliver the Site, keep it secure and fix faults.

3.5 Information from public sources

To prepare homepage previews and contact businesses that may benefit from a new website, we collect business information that has been made publicly available, such as a business’s name, website address and content, publicly listed business email address and phone number, and the names and roles of people publicly listed as contacts for that business. Section 5 explains how we use it.

3.6 Information we do not want

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send us, sensitive information such as health information, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, political opinions, sexual orientation, criminal records, or government identifiers such as passport, driver licence or tax file numbers.

3.7 If you do not give us information

You do not have to give us any personal information. If you do not provide your name and email address, we will not be able to reply to your enquiry. You can contact us without using your real name, but we will usually need to know who you are before we can take on work for you.

5. Business outreach and homepage previews

Part of how we find clients is by preparing a homepage preview for a business and sending it to that business. To do this, we use the publicly available information described in section 3.5.

When we contact a business:

  • we contact only publicly listed business contact details, and only about services relevant to that business;
  • every message identifies Lanterno Studio and tells you how to reach us;
  • every message gives you a simple way to tell us you do not want to hear from us again; and
  • if you opt out, we will stop contacting you within five business days, and keep a minimal record of your email address so that we do not contact you again.

Homepage previews are shared privately with the business they were made for and are not published or promoted. If you would like a preview made for your business deleted, ask us and we will delete it.

To opt out, reply to any of our messages or email hello@lanternostudio.com.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We disclose it only to the following kinds of recipients, and only as much as they need:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, including our website hosting provider (Vercel), our network and security provider (Cloudflare), the infrastructure that runs our workflow automation system (which receives contact form submissions), our email and file storage providers, and our payment and banking providers. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
  • Providers involved in delivering your project, such as your domain registrar or hosting provider, when we act on your instructions.
  • Professional advisers, such as accountants, lawyers and insurers, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Authorities and others where the law requires or permits it, such as to comply with a court order or to protect someone’s safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold, restructured or transferred, in which case your information remains subject to the protections in this policy.

7. International transfers

We are based in Australia, and some of our service providers store or process information in other countries, including the United States and other countries where they or their sub-processors operate. This means your personal information may be disclosed to, or stored in, countries other than the one you live in.

Before disclosing personal information overseas, we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles it in a way that is consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles and this policy. Where the GDPR applies to a transfer, we rely on an adequacy decision, the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or another safeguard permitted by law. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

8. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, or as long as the law requires. In general:

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: up to 24 months after our last contact with you.
  • Outreach records, including homepage previews: up to 24 months after our last contact with the business, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Opt-out records: for as long as we need them to make sure we do not contact you again.
  • Client records: for the length of the engagement and then for up to seven years, so we can meet our tax, accounting and legal obligations.
  • Access details for your accounts: only until the work that needs them is finished. See section 12.
  • Server logs: for the limited periods set by our hosting and network providers.

When we no longer need personal information, we delete it or permanently de-identify it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. These include encrypting information in transit, limiting access to the people who need it, using strong unique passwords and multi-factor authentication on our accounts, and storing access details for client accounts in an encrypted password manager.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm to you, we will notify you and the relevant regulator where the law requires us to, and within the time it requires.

10. Your rights

10.1 Everyone

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you whether we hold personal information about you, and give you access to it;
  • correct information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading;
  • delete your information, unless we need to keep it to meet a legal obligation or to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim; and
  • stop sending you business communications.

10.2 If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area

In addition, you have the right to:

  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to object at any time, without needing a reason, to the use of your information for direct marketing;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information;
  • receive information you gave us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and have it sent to another organisation where technically feasible;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent, without affecting processing that took place before you withdrew it; and
  • complain to a data protection supervisory authority (see section 14).

10.3 If you are in the United States

Depending on the state you live in, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to access, correct and delete it; and to opt out of its sale, of its sharing for targeted advertising, and of profiling. We do not sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising or use it for profiling. You may use an authorised agent to make a request on your behalf, and you may appeal if we decline to act on your request by replying to our decision. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your rights.

10.4 How to make a request

Email hello@lanternostudio.com. We may need to confirm your identity, or an agent’s authority, before we act on a request. We will respond within 30 days. If a request is complex, we may need longer, as permitted by the law that applies to you, and we will tell you why. We do not charge for requests unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive, in which case we may charge a reasonable fee or decline, and we will explain our reasons. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why in writing and how you can complain.

11. Cookies and tracking

The Site does not use cookies, web beacons, analytics tools, advertising pixels or similar tracking technologies, and it does not store information in your browser. Our typefaces are served from our own website, so viewing the Site does not send your information to font providers.

Because we do not track you across websites, we do not need to respond to “Do Not Track” signals. If you send a Global Privacy Control signal, we will treat it as a request to opt out of any sale or sharing of your personal information, which we do not carry out in any case.

If we introduce cookies or analytics in the future, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

12. If you are a client

Access to your accounts. To launch your website, you may give us access to your domain registrar, hosting or other accounts. We use that access only to carry out the work you have asked for. Where possible, please give us our own user access rather than sharing your login details. When the work is finished, we will delete any login details you gave us and ask you to remove our access or change your passwords.

Personal information on your website. Your website may collect personal information from your own customers, for example through a contact form. You are responsible for that information and for having your own privacy policy that describes it. If we handle that information while building or supporting your website, we do so only on your instructions and only for that purpose. If you need a data processing agreement, ask us and we will provide one.

13. Other things you should know

Children. Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites. The Site and our communications may link to other websites, including websites we have built for clients. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Changes to this policy. We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the law or in how we work. We will publish the updated policy on this page with a new effective date, and if a change materially affects how we handle your personal information, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.

14. Questions and complaints

If you have a question about this policy, or a concern or complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first at hello@lanternostudio.com. Please tell us what happened and how you would like it resolved. We will acknowledge your complaint, look into it, and give you a written response within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the regulator where you live, including:

  • Australia: the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, oaic.gov.au
  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner’s Office, ico.org.uk
  • European Economic Area: the data protection supervisory authority in the country where you live or work, or where the issue occurred
  • United States: the Attorney General or privacy regulator of your state

In the UK and the European Economic Area, you may complain to a supervisory authority at any time, without contacting us first.
