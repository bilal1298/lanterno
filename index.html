Fair
questions.

How long does it take to launch my website?

If you’re happy with your homepage preview as it is, and you send access to your domain and hosting along with payment, we usually launch within 1–2 business days.

If you’d like changes, or access takes a while to sort out, the timeline depends on that back-and-forth, so there’s no fixed timeframe.

Who writes the content?

We do. We build your whole website with content written for your business and optimised for search engines. If you’d like anything reworded, added or taken out, just ask and we’ll change it.

Do I need my own domain and hosting?

You’ll need a domain, and it stays in your name. If you’re comfortable giving us access to your domain and hosting, and your hosting can run our websites, we’ll handle everything.

If you’d rather not share access to your domain, we’ll send you a step-by-step guide for your domain registrar. Our websites are built with Next.js; if your current hosting can’t run it, you can buy compatible hosting and give us access, or host with us.

Are there any monthly fees?

No mandatory ones. You pay US$995 once and own the finished website. Domain registration, paid third-party tools and optional managed hosting are separate.

How does payment work?

Once we’ve agreed the scope, we take a 50% deposit and complete your website. The remaining 50% is paid before launch, and the finished website is yours once it’s paid.

What if I don’t like the homepage preview?

Then you don’t owe us anything. A preview comes with no obligation. If you like the direction but want changes, tell us what you have in mind and we’ll take it from there.

Do you work with businesses outside Australia?

Yes. We work with businesses around the world, including the US, the UK and Australia.
